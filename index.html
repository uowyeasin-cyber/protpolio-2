About

Hi, I'm Yeasin. I build software and design things people actually use.

I'm studying a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science at the University of Wollongong Malaysia, on the UOW High Achievers Scholarship for outstanding academic results. I scored a perfect GPA of 5.00 in both my Dakhil (SSC) and Intermediate (HSC) exams. On campus I'm in the Computer Science Club and take part in programming competitions and hackathons.

I founded Averroes AI, where I build AI-powered tools, automation and practical software. My flagship app is Amalnama, a free, ad-free Islamic companion app that works offline in six languages.

Before university I founded Hidayah Blood Bank in 2017 to connect patients with nearby donors, then the Hidayah United Foundation, a non-profit. During COVID I started Hidayah Shop BD, an online store that I restarted after my HSC and ran until 2025. I also interned at Programming Hero and Code Biruni and trained students in English and IELTS.

As a freelance designer, my strongest work is in food and retail. For The Halal Gyro Spot in Pensacola, Florida, I designed the logo, a six-page menu, a Z-fold brochure, an LED menu board, launch posts, an ordering app concept and promo videos.

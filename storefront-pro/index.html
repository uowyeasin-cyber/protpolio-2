How it works

From download to open shop

  1. Download the free demo. Inside is START-HERE.md: a step-by-step guide in Bangla (and English) with every link.
  2. Make free GitHub and Vercel accounts, upload the files, connect the free database. Your shop opens at yourname.vercel.app.
  3. Sign in to the admin, pick a theme, add your products, payments and couriers.
  4. Pick a plan and message me. You get a license key; paste it into Vercel as LICENSE_KEY.
  5. Optional: buy your own .com and connect it. The guide shows how.

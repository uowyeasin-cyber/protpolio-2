FAQ

Common questions

Is Easy Life free?

Yes. The Free plan is free forever, and the first 14 days include every Pro feature. After that you can stay free or pick Pro or Lifetime.

Does it work in Bangla?

Yes. Speak Bangla, English or a mix. You can also switch the app's language any time in Settings.

Do I need the internet?

Notes, filing and reminders work without it. AI summaries, reading photos and reading links need the internet.

Do I need an AI key?

No. Pro includes AI. On the Free plan you can add your own free Gemini or Groq key if you like.

Can it send messages for me?

It writes the message, translates it if needed and opens WhatsApp, SMS or a call to the right person. Android asks you to press the final Send yourself, so nothing goes out by mistake.

Is there an iPhone version?

Android only for now (version 8 or newer). iPhone may come later.

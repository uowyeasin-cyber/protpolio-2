What's inside

Every part of life, sorted for you

🎙️

Accurate voice notes

Bangla, English or both mixed. Photos and links become notes too, and the AI files each one into the right life area.

⏰

Its own reminder engine

Notification, alarm even on silent, or a full-screen call-style alert. Works offline and after restart, never duplicates.

🎓

Class mode

Records the whole lecture with board photos. Mark "didn't get it" and get a simple Bangla explanation and a quiz.

💳

Automatic money tracking

Counts bKash, Nagad and bank payments from notifications. Several businesses, dues and who owes what.

✨

One-tap create

Pick message, intro, email or CV. The AI suggests who to send it to and why, writes it, and sends only when you tap.

💬

Inbox & auto-reply

WhatsApp, Messenger, page comments and Gmail in one place. Replies for you when busy, with your permission.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family circle

Shared duties like mum's medicine, rent and tuition fees, a group chat that turns talk into tasks, and an easy mode for parents.

📖

My life story

One small question each night builds your biography: chapters from birth to now and the people who shaped you. Export as a PDF book.

FAQ

Common questions

Which payment methods can my store accept?

Cards and FPX through Stripe, PayPal, and bKash, Nagad and cards in Bangladesh through SSLCommerz, plus cash on delivery. Orders are marked paid only after the payment provider confirms them.

Can you connect a courier?

Yes. Sunnah Store books Steadfast deliveries in Bangladesh and tracks international parcels automatically, and I can set up the same for your shop.

Do I need to know coding to run the store?

No. You manage products, prices, orders and offers from an admin panel.
